# Footer store badges

Downloaded 2026-10-24. Use the original artwork without recoloring, cropping, or extra background frames.

- `app-store-en.svg`: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
- `google-play-en.svg` and `google-play-ar.svg`: official **Google Play Badge guidelines.zip**, Digital/svg/GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg and Digital/svg/GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_Arabic-Saudi-Arabia.svg from https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/?folder=76635

The App Store badge uses Apple's English artwork in both site languages; accessible text remains localized. Google Play artwork follows the current site language. Each badge displays at 40px high with its original aspect ratio. Thin outlines are part of the official artwork; there is no additional grey rectangle.

Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
